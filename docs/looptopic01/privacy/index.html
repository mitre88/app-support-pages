Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

LoopTopic01 is designed around daily micro-challenges and keeps your usage data local to the app by default.

If enabled, optional notifications and haptics are used only for reminders and feedback. No personal health or financial data is collected.

LoopTopic01 does not include analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or behavioral ad-tracking integrations.

You can request deletion of your local app data by deleting the app from your device. Backup behavior remains controlled by iCloud/Apple device settings.
