Unseat

Support

Unseat is designed for brief, low-impact movement breaks during a desk-based workday.

Quick help

  • Change reminders or Apple Health access from Unseat Settings.
  • Use Seated only mode when standing movements are not appropriate.
  • Stop immediately if a movement causes discomfort, dizziness, shortness of breath, or unusual symptoms.
  • Unseat does not diagnose conditions, correct posture, treat pain, or replace professional medical advice.

For support, email us with the app version and iOS version. Do not email health records or other sensitive information.
