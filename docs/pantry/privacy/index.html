Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Pantry does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Fridge inventory, saved meals, grocery items, language selection, notification preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Optional photos you pick to help add ingredients stay on the device for that review step and are not uploaded. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional local reminders at 7:00 and 19:00 can be disabled in Settings or in iOS system settings. They are scheduled on-device.

If Apple Intelligence is available on your device, it may rewrite a short on-screen line. That processing stays on device. The meal list is always computed locally even when Apple Intelligence is off.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
