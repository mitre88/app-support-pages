Pantry

Support

Pantry is an offline kitchen helper. Photograph or type what is in the fridge, get three meal cards from those ingredients, and build a grocery list of only the missing items.

Quick help

  • Add ingredients from the catalog or with a photo. Confirm every suggested item before it enters the fridge.
  • Open Meals to see three dinners computed on this iPhone. Tap a card to cook it or send the gap to Groceries.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Everything stays on this device. Deleting the app removes inventory, meals, and lists.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
