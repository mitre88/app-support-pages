Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Pomodoro AI does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Focus sessions, cycle presets, theme and sound preferences, notification preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes these records.

Focus Insights are computed on this iPhone from your own session history. When an on-device model is available, it writes the insight text on device; otherwise a local rule-based summary is used. Your sessions never leave the device.

The Live Activity and Home Screen widgets read the running timer and session summary from local storage only. Cycle-end notifications are scheduled on-device.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
