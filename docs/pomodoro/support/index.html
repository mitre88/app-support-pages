Pomodoro AI

Support

Pomodoro AI is a focus timer. Run 25/5 cycles or build your own, keep the timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and read on-device Focus Insights about the hours you actually focus.

Quick help

  • Today starts a focus cycle. Break and focus lengths are in Settings.
  • The Live Activity shows the running timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Turn it off in Settings if you prefer.
  • Notifications fire when a cycle ends. Allow them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Pomodoro AI.
  • Focus Insights are generated on this iPhone from your own session history. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Pomodoro AI Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Reinstalled the app or changed phones? Use Restore purchase in Settings.
  • Home Screen widgets and premium themes are part of Pro.
  • Deleting the app removes every session and insight.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
