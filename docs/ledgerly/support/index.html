Ledgerly

Support

Ledgerly is a private receipt ledger. Photograph a receipt or type a line, confirm merchant and category, and see a weekly spend brief on this iPhone. It does not log into a bank.

Quick help

  • Use Capture to photograph a receipt or add a line by hand. Confirm merchant, amount, and category before saving.
  • Today shows this week’s ring. Ledger lists every receipt. Insights groups the week by category.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Everything stays on this device. Deleting the app removes the ledger.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
