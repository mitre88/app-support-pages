Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Ledgerly does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It is not a bank and does not connect to bank accounts. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Receipts, categories, language selection, notification preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Photos you take or pick for a receipt are read on device and are not uploaded. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional local reminders at 7:00 and 19:00 can be disabled in Settings or in iOS system settings.

If Apple Intelligence is available, it may suggest a merchant name on device. You always confirm the saved line.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
