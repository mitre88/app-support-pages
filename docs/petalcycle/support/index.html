PetalCycle

Support

PetalCycle is a private cycle tracker that presents date ranges and uncertainty instead of claiming a confirmed ovulation day.

Quick help

  • Add or edit a day from Today or Calendar.
  • Predictions become more representative as additional period starts are logged.
  • LH, cervical mucus, and basal temperature are stored as context in v1; they do not confirm ovulation or move the estimate.
  • Do not use PetalCycle to prevent pregnancy.
  • Seek qualified care for severe pain, very heavy bleeding, a suspected pregnancy, or cycle changes that concern you.

For support, email us with the app version and iOS version. Do not email reproductive-health records or other sensitive information.
