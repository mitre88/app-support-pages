ClearTone · One-tap sound

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy describes ClearTone for iPhone, developed by Alex Mitre. One central Start/Stop button controls a 20-second cycle with four selectable tones and optional native vibration. Playback works without an account or an internet connection.

ClearTone support All support pages

Information used on your iPhone

ClearTone generates its four tone presets and finite haptic patterns locally. It checks whether the device supports native vibration. It does not access the microphone, record or analyze sound, or measure moisture or dirt. It reads the audio output and audio-session state to manage playback, and uses system uptime to measure elapsed cycle time. Accessibility, power and thermal state are used locally to adapt presentation and playback.

Audio buffers, haptic patterns, the selected tone, vibration preference and cycle state are held in memory, not saved as a history or uploaded. This temporary state is discarded when the app process ends. Switching to another app stops sound and vibration but does not necessarily end the app process.

No app-operated data collection

The app has no server connection, cloud sync, analytics or advertising SDKs, tracking identifiers, accounts or embedded web views. It does not send playback activity or device state to the developer. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. There is no stored app account or activity history to request or delete.

Web pages and external links

Privacy, support and Apple guidance links open only when you choose them. Your browser and the destination site then handle the web request under their own privacy practices; see Apple’s privacy information.

These public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, separately from the app. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in its Pages data-collection notice and privacy statement. The ClearTone pages themselves add no scripts, forms, cookies or analytics code.

Optional email and deletion requests

Choosing a support email link opens your email app; it does not send a message automatically. If you email support@alexmitre.com, your address, message and any attachments become support correspondence used to respond to your request. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Correspondence can remain in the developer’s mailbox and email providers’ storage after a reply. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised here. To request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence, contact the same address and identify the relevant message. Email providers may retain delivery records or backups under their own policies; deleting correspondence does not guarantee immediate removal of those provider records.
