Start or stop the sound

Choose a tone, then tap the central Start button for a 20-second cycle through the built-in speaker. Choose Deep (160 Hz), Bright (260 Hz), Sweep (160–320 Hz) or Pulse (220 Hz, two pulses per second). The button becomes Stop, so you can end playback at any time. A full cycle fades in and out and finishes automatically.

Turn on Vibration to accompany the tone with native device vibration on supported iPhones. Sound and vibration use different hardware; the tone frequency does not set the vibration motor’s frequency. On unsupported hardware, the switch is unavailable and sound remains usable. Changing the tone or vibration setting stops the current cycle and waits for another tap on Start.

  • Choose a comfortable, moderate volume with your iPhone’s system controls. Keep the speaker away from ears and stop if you notice discomfort or distortion. ClearTone does not change system volume.
  • Sound can play while Silent Mode is on. Warn people nearby before starting.
  • Disconnect headphones, Bluetooth and USB audio, and turn off AirPlay. Playback is limited to the built-in speaker.
  • Leaving the app, an interruption or an output-route change stops sound and vibration. Neither resumes automatically.

A completed cycle does not prove cleaning or water removal. ClearTone does not measure dirt, moisture or sound pressure, diagnose damage, certify dryness or guarantee any improvement.

If sound is unavailable

Read the status message, disconnect audio accessories and check system volume. If vibration cannot start, try again or turn Vibration off to use sound alone. If ClearTone asks you to close the app before restarting, do so. Do not keep repeating cycles to try to fix a persistent problem.

If the iPhone may be wet or damaged

Do not charge a wet phone or deliberately wet one to test ClearTone. Follow Apple’s liquid-exposure and drying guidance. Sound is not a substitute for drying. Unusual heat, swelling or visible damage needs professional support.

For continuing muffled or distorted sound, follow Apple’s speaker troubleshooting guidance and seek qualified service.

Offline use and privacy

The sound cycle works offline. ClearTone has no microphone access, account, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Playback state is temporary and is not saved as a history. Public links need a connection when you open them. Read the ClearTone privacy policy for web-hosting and optional-email details.
