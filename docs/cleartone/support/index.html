Before using the sound cycle

The optional cycle is an experimental 8-second tone, not a repair or a proven water-removal method. ClearTone cannot detect moisture, diagnose damage, confirm cleaning or tell you that a phone is dry. Elapsed time is not a cleaning score.

  • Read and acknowledge the precautions before each cycle. Nothing starts automatically.
  • Disconnect headphones, Bluetooth and USB audio, and turn off AirPlay. ClearTone checks for the built-in speaker before playback.
  • Keep the phone away from ears. Choose a comfortable, moderate system volume and stop immediately on discomfort or distortion. Actual sound pressure has not been measured.
  • Silent Mode does not mute this cycle. ClearTone does not change system volume. Warn people nearby before starting.
  • Use Stop sound to end a cycle. Leaving the app, audio interruptions or route changes cancel playback without automatic resumption.

If sound is unavailable

Read the status message. Disconnect audio accessories and review the precautions again before a new attempt. If the app asks you to close it before replaying because the audio session was not released, close the app first. If the device is too warm, let it cool. Do not keep repeating experiments to try to fix persistent sound problems.

Drying and exterior care

Do not charge a wet phone or deliberately wet one to test ClearTone. Give it time and airflow; do not use external heat, compressed air or objects inside openings. Follow Apple’s liquid-exposure and drying guidance. Sound is not a substitute for drying.

The separate Dust & dirt guide explains gentle exterior care; its checkmarks mean instructions were reviewed, not that cleaning happened. Sound is not a method for removing adhered dirt. For continuing muffled or distorted sound, follow Apple’s speaker troubleshooting guidance and seek qualified service.

Offline use and privacy

ClearTone has no account, microphone recording, in-app purchases or subscriptions. The tone and guide work locally; Apple links need a browser connection only when opened. Guide progress is temporary and is not saved between app processes. For web-hosting and voluntary-email details, including deletion requests, read the ClearTone privacy policy.
