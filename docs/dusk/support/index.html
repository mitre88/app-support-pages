Dusk

Support

Dusk is a digital sunset ritual. Set an evening hour, walk a short shutdown checklist, write tomorrow’s three, and let the interface dim. It is not meditation audio and it does not block other apps.

Quick help

  • Set sunset time in Settings. Tonight shows the checklist and tomorrow’s three.
  • Week shows which evenings you completed. Nothing here is a Screen Time report.
  • Turn off dim-after-sunset if you want the daytime palette at night.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
