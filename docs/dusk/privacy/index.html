Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Dusk does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Sunset time, checklist progress, tomorrow’s three, night logs, language selection, notification preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional local reminders at 7:00 and 19:00 can be disabled in Settings or in iOS system settings. They are scheduled on-device.

Dusk does not enable Screen Time restrictions, does not read other apps, and does not record audio.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
