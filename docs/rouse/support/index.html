Rouse

Support

Rouse is an alarm clock. It is not sleep therapy and does not replace Clock.app medical or accessibility alarms.

Quick help

  • Create a named alarm with time and repeat days.
  • Dismiss or snooze when it rings. One snooze rule, on purpose.
  • Today can show a short morning brief after you get up.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes saved alarms.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
