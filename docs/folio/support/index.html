Folio

Support

Folio is a private writing desk. Open a page, write, optionally rewrite on this iPhone, and keep a weekly brief. It is not a mood tracker.

Quick help

  • Today opens a new page. Title, body, and tags stay on this iPhone.
  • Rewrite is a suggestion. Accept it or keep the original.
  • The weekly brief uses the last seven pages. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes every page.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
