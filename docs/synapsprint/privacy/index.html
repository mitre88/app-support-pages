SynapSprint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

SynapSprint does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Progress, best scores, reaction stats, language selection, sound/haptics preferences, comfort cadence, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional on-device Apple Intelligence word generation runs only when available and never sends your data to a remote service from this app. Gameplay always falls back to curated offline word banks.

The app does not access health records, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, Bluetooth, or motion data.
