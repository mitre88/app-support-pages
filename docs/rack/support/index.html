Rack

Support

Rack is a private gym logger: last-time weights, rest timer, and weekly volume. No social feed.

Quick help

  • Start a session from Today and log sets as you lift.
  • Last-time values fill in from your history on this iPhone.
  • History shows volume. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes workout history.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
