Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Rack does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Workouts, sets, custom exercises, language selection, notification preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Optional HealthKit workouts are written only if you allow Health access. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional local reminders at 7:00 and 19:00 can be disabled in Settings or in iOS system settings. They are scheduled on-device.

If Apple Intelligence is available on your device, it may write a short session note from the lifts you logged. That processing stays on device. Rack still works with bundled copy when Apple Intelligence is off.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
