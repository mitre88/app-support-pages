Poise

Support

Poise is a posture check-in timer with a 20-second reset. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat injury.

Quick help

  • Today shows your score and the next check-in.
  • Start a 20-second reset when the reminder fires, or start one yourself.
  • History is a private calendar of completed check-ins.
  • Change language in Settings. English, Spanish, and French are included.
  • Turn off 7:00 and 19:00 reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes check-in history.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
