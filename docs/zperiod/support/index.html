The periodic table

  • Pinch to zoom and drag to move around the table. Double-tap to go back to the whole table.
  • Search by name, symbol or atomic number, or filter by element category to focus on one group.
  • Tap an element to open its atom in 3D. Rotate it with one finger and pull up the info card to read about the element.

The 3D labs

Eight animated labs show bonds and reactions step by step.

  • Play, pause and drag the timeline to any moment.
  • Rotate the molecules with one finger. The caption explains each step.

Language, theme and tours

Open Settings to switch between Spanish, English and French, choose light, dark or the system theme, and replay the intro or the guided tour. Settings also shows the installed version, the licenses and the privacy policy.

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo

Zperiod needs iOS 18 or later. On iPad, and on iPhone Duo when it is open, the list and the atom or lab show side by side.

Offline use and purchases

Zperiod works 100% offline and has no account to sign in to. It is a paid app sold by the App Store: to install it on another device, download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Zperiod collects no data. Read the Zperiod privacy policy.
