Anatomy Studio: 3D Atlas

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Anatomy Studio: 3D Atlas does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, backend, or third-party network client.

Favorites, lesson progress, language selection, notification preferences, onboarding status, and notes are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes these records.

Optional local study reminder notifications can be disabled in Settings or in iOS system settings. They are scheduled on-device and no data leaves the device.

The app does not access health records, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, Bluetooth, motion data, or Apple Intelligence.
