BioClase 3D: Study & Teach

Support

BioClase 3D is a study-and-teach companion for the human-body topics of Mexico's first-year secundaria Biology course. It links learning goals to 3D organ models, practice with spaced review, and a classroom presentation mode.

Quick help

  • Program: open a topic, choose a learning goal, then Open in 3D, Practice, or Present in class.
  • Review: cards you practiced come back on a spaced schedule; the tab shows how many are due today.
  • Class: step through a short guided sequence on an iPad or a projector. AirPlay or HDMI mirroring, a keyboard, or a presentation clicker can advance the steps.
  • Language: the app follows your device language. To change it only for BioClase, open iOS Settings → BioClase → Language.
  • Start over: use Delete my progress on the About screen, for example on a shared class iPad.

Independent app. Not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Mexico's Secretaría de Educación Pública (SEP).

Ayuda (español)

  • Programa: abre un tema, elige un objetivo de aprendizaje y toca Abrir en 3D, Practicar o Presentar en clase.
  • Repaso: las tarjetas que practicaste regresan con repaso espaciado; la pestaña muestra cuántas tocan hoy.
  • Clase: avanza una secuencia guiada breve en iPad o proyector, con AirPlay o HDMI, teclado o presentador.
  • Idioma: la app sigue el idioma del dispositivo. Para cambiarlo solo en BioClase: Ajustes → BioClase → Idioma.
  • Empezar de nuevo: usa Borrar mi avance en Acerca de, por ejemplo en un iPad compartido del salón.

App independiente. No está afiliada, patrocinada ni avalada por la SEP.
