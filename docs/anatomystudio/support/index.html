Anatomy Studio: 3D Atlas

Support

Anatomy Studio: 3D Atlas is an offline anatomy learning app with twelve interactive 3D organ models, guided lessons, quizzes, comparison, and private on-device notes.

Quick help

  • Pick an organ from the library, then rotate and zoom the 3D model with standard gestures.
  • Switch between surface, layers, wireframe, and cross-section views from the specimen controls.
  • Change the app language anytime in Settings; seven languages are included.
  • Disable optional study reminders in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Your notes and favorites are stored only on your device; deleting the app removes them.

Contact: support@alexmitre.com with the iOS version and app version.
